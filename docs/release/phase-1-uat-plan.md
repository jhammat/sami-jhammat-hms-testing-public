# WonFlow Phase 1 UAT Plan

UAT covers Reception patient registration, duplicate review, appointments, cancellation, check-in and queue tokens; Hospital and Platform Administration configuration; Doctor encounters, observations, diagnoses, notes, orders and prescriptions; Patient self-service; Laboratory and Radiology result workflows; Pharmacy dispensing; Billing payments and refunds; and Patient/Doctor Android installation, secure sessions, notifications, offline recovery and accessibility.

Every case records tester, date, tenant, branch, device/browser, expected and actual results, evidence, severity, resolution, and sign-off. Reception’s approved visual design must remain unchanged.

No area is accepted until its tenant/branch isolation, empty states, permission failures, concurrency behavior and audit evidence are verified.
